Model Overview & Specifications

Built for fast and reliable computer-aided screening of Giemsa-stained microscopic blood smears.

MobileNetV2 Backbone

Uses lightweight depthwise separable convolutions pre-trained on ImageNet, providing high feature extraction quality with minimal latency.

Two-Phase Training

Initial training mapped base features to cell categories, followed by unfreezing deeper layers with a low learning rate (1e-5) to specialize on ring trophozoites.

95.81% Validation Accuracy

Evaluated on an independent validation split of thin blood smear images with balanced sensitivity across parasitized and uninfected samples.

Standardized Preprocessing

All inputs are centered, resized to 128×128 pixels, and normalized into [-1.0, 1.0] tensor format to ensure consistency across stains.
